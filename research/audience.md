# Target Audience

## Audience

Bid, proposal, and enterprise sales teams at India-headquartered digital engineering and IT-services companies selling complex technology transformation work to enterprise customers.

This is deliberately narrower than "IT companies." These organizations repeatedly need to explain technical capabilities, delivery approaches, industry experience, case studies, scope, and implementation plans to different enterprise buyers.

## Why this audience

The hypothesis is that these teams often begin with existing approved material and repeatedly adapt it for a specific opportunity.

That creates a strong potential fit for SuperDocs because the workflow is fundamentally document editing rather than blank-page content generation.

The machine therefore tests one specific growth hypothesis:

> Can public company-level signals identify enterprise technology-services firms where proposal-document customization is likely to be a meaningful workflow?

## What they likely do today

Possible current workflows include:

- approved Microsoft Word proposal templates
- reusable capability and case-study libraries
- manual copy/paste between previous proposals
- subject-matter expert contributions
- repeated section-level rewriting
- human review before submission

These are hypotheses rather than claims about any company's internal process.

## What could make them switch

The value proposition tested by the machine is not autonomous proposal creation.

It is:

- start with an existing approved document
- adapt selected sections for the opportunity
- preserve document structure
- reduce repetitive editing
- retain human review and approval
